Harmony Music School 🎵
Welcome to Harmony Music School, an online platform where music enthusiasts can explore, learn, and master the art of music. Built with Next.js, this application offers a seamless user experience, providing access to high-quality courses, engaging articles, and an easy way to connect with us.

Features ✨
1. Courses 🎸🎹
Discover a variety of music courses designed for beginners and advanced learners alike. Each course is crafted to enhance your skills in instruments, vocals, theory, and more.

2. Music Articles 📚
Stay inspired with our curated collection of music-related articles. From tips and techniques to the history of music, there's something for every music lover.

3. Contact Us ✉️
Have questions or need support? Reach out to us through the Contact Us page for prompt assistance.

Technologies Used 🛠️
Next.js: Server-side rendering and static site generation for fast and efficient web performance.
React: Building interactive and dynamic user interfaces.
Node.js: Backend support for API handling.
CSS/Styled Components: Ensuring a visually appealing and responsive design.
